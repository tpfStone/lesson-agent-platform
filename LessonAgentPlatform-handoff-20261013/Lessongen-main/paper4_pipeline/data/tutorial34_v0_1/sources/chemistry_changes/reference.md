# 工程测试参考：物理变化与化学变化 / Physical and Chemical Changes

## 中文

- 是否生成新物质是区分物理变化和化学变化的核心依据。
- 状态或形状改变而物质本身不变，通常属于物理变化。
- 颜色变化、气体、沉淀、发光或温度变化可作为化学变化线索，但线索必须结合物质与过程解释。
- 课堂观察活动不得让学生品尝未知物质，也不应安排超出学校安全条件的操作。

## English

- Formation of new substances is the central basis for distinguishing chemical from physical change.
- A change in state or shape without a new substance is usually physical.
- Color change, gas, precipitate, light, or temperature change can be clues, but each clue needs interpretation in context.
- Classroom observation must not involve tasting unknown substances or unsafe procedures.
