# 工程测试参考：一般过去时 / Simple Past Tense

## 中文

- 一般过去时描述过去发生并结束的动作或状态，常与明确的过去时间语境搭配。
- 规则动词通常加 `-ed`，不规则动词需要单独识记。
- `be` 动词根据主语使用 `was` 或 `were`。
- 一般动词的否定句和疑问句通常使用 `did`，主要动词恢复原形。
- 课堂交际任务应关注意义表达，不能只做脱离语境的词形变化。

## English

- The simple past describes completed past actions or states and commonly appears with a clear past-time context.
- Regular verbs commonly take `-ed`; irregular forms need separate attention.
- The verb `be` changes to `was` or `were` according to the subject.
- Negatives and questions with ordinary verbs commonly use `did`, with the main verb in its base form.
- A communicative task should preserve meaning and not reduce learning to isolated form changes.
