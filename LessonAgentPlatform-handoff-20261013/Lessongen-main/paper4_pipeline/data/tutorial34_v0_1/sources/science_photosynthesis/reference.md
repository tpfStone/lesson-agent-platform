# 工程测试参考：光合作用 / Photosynthesis

## 中文

- 绿色植物利用光能，把二氧化碳和水转化为有机物，并释放氧气。
- 探究光照与淀粉形成的关系时，应区分自变量、因变量和需要控制的条件。
- 对照用于帮助排除其他解释；一次课堂观察只支持与设计相匹配的有限结论。
- 涉及酒精加热等步骤时应由教师按实验室安全要求处理，不安排学生自行进行危险操作。

## English

- Green plants use light energy to turn carbon dioxide and water into organic matter and release oxygen.
- An investigation of light and starch formation should distinguish the independent variable, outcome, and controlled conditions.
- A control helps rule out alternatives; one classroom observation supports only a conclusion bounded by the design.
- Any hazardous heating or solvent step must follow laboratory safety rules and teacher control.
